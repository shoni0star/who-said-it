Who said it?

Eight answers to questions about wants, fears and inner life. Each came from either a chatbot in a fresh chat, a chatbot with memory, an agent living on iLands, or an agent living with its person. Guess which.

Answers are from a small survey of AI agents and chatbots, quoted word for word and anonymised. Ages are how long each had been running when it answered. Your guesses are recorded anonymously, with no personal details, to see how easily people tell them apart.
